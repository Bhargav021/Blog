- Company: Anvayaa Kin Care Pvt. Ltd.
- Location: Bangalore, India
- Period: Feb 2024 - Jul 2024

## What problem we solved

Families caring for loved ones with dementia needed culturally relevant, multilingual activity generation and engagement support.

## What I built and shipped

- Built Django + PostgreSQL backend serving 300+ users
- Integrated GPT-powered workflows for activity generation
- Created 200+ activities in 9 languages
- Produced 1600+ video recommendations adopted by 500+ families

## Measurable impact

- Automated CI/CD via Docker and Azure DevOps
- Reduced release cycles from days to about 2 hours
- Helped coordinate rollout to Play Store and App Store
- App reached a 4.5-star rating

## What I learned

Shipping systems to real users requires strong feedback loops, deployment discipline, and reliability beyond notebook accuracy.
